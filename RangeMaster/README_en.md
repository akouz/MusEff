# RangeMaster

Practical implementation of overdrive similar to [Dallas Rangemaster](https://github.com/akouz/MusEff/blob/main/Assymetrical/README.md#5-dallas-rangemaster).

![Fig 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/akouz/MusEff/refs/heads/main/RangeMaster/RangeMaster_rev_1_2.png)

The ON-OFF-ON switch SW1 adjusts the high-pass filter cutoff frequency at the input of the distortion stage Q1. The tone control RV2 is similar to that found on the Big Muff Pi.

The ON-OFF-ON switch SW2 was designed to emulate the properties of the original Dallas Rangemaster. If a pedal is connected before the input, SW2 has no effect. It operates when the guitar is connected directly to the pedal's input. In the extreme positions of SW2, capacitors C20 and C21 interact with the guitar pickup's inductance, creating resonance. The effect is similar to that of a wah-wah pedal connected between the guitar and the pedal with the pedal in a fixed position.

Sound examples: TONE at 12:00, SW1 at the bottom position (as per the diagram).
